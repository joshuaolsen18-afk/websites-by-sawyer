Questions, answered straight

How long does a simple site take?

A one-page landing site can be a few hours. A 4–5 page business site is usually 1–3 days of build time, once we have your info. Revisions and domain setup can stretch the calendar to about a week — the build isn't the slow part, waiting on photos and wording is.

Do I need photos and a logo first?

Photos help a lot — the demos above prove it. A logo is nice, but not required to start. If you don't have photos yet, tell us and we'll plan around it, or use clean placeholder sections until you do.

Will my site work on a phone?

Yes — that's the priority. Most customers will find you on a phone, so every site is built mobile-first and checked on a phone screen before launch.

Can I update it later?

Yes. Send us the new menu, hours, or photo and we'll update it. You don't have to learn a builder, and you don't have to start over.
